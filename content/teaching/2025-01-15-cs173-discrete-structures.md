Teaching Assistant for CS173: Discrete Structures at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Spring 2025.

**Course Description:**
CS173 covers fundamental discrete mathematics topics essential for computer science, including logic, proofs, sets, functions, relations, combinatorics, graph theory, and formal languages. The course emphasizes mathematical reasoning and proof techniques.
