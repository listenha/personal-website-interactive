Teaching Assistant for CS225: Data Structures at Zhejiang University, Spring 2024.

**Course Description:**
CS225 covers fundamental data structures and their applications in computer science, including arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, trees, graphs, and hash tables. The course emphasizes algorithm analysis, time and space complexity, and practical implementation of data structures.

**Responsibilities:**
- Led weekly discussion sections and office hours
- Graded assignments and exams
- Provided one-on-one tutoring and support for students
- Assisted in developing course materials and practice problems
