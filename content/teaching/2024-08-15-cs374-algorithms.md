Teaching Assistant for CS374: Introduction to Algorithms & Models of Computation at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Fall 2024.

**Course Description:**
CS374 introduces fundamental algorithms and computational models, covering topics such as algorithm design techniques (divide-and-conquer, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms), graph algorithms, computational complexity, and models of computation including finite automata and Turing machines.

**Responsibilities:**
- Conducted weekly discussion sections covering problem-solving strategies
- Held regular office hours to assist students with homework and exam preparation
- Graded assignments, exams, and programming projects
- Collaborated with course staff to develop practice problems and review materials
