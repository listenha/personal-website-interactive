Hi! I'm Yueshen Li, a CS PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign working with Prof. [Dilek Hakkani-Tur](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/about/people/faculty/dilek) and Prof. [Koustuv Saha](https://koustuv.com/). I'm broadly interested in **Human–AI Interaction**, **LLM Reasoning & Agents Harness**, and **AI systems that understand people**. My work spans **H-AI tacit understanding/cooridination, behavioral signal analysis, and LLM-based agents**, and I build full-stack systems to study how humans and AI collaborate in structured/unstructured settings. My most recent focus is on self-evolving agents for industrial-intent tasks.

Before pivoting into HCI/HAI, I worked in security and networked systems, including leading **CAPSID**, a privacy-preserving UAV identification protocol published at **ACM CCS 2024**, and collaborating over **Automatic FarmNetes**, which led to a publication at **ACM SEC 2025**.
