Hi! I'm a CS PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign working with Prof. [Dilek Hakkani-Tur](https://siebelschool.illinois.edu/about/people/faculty/dilek) and Prof. [Koustuv Saha](https://koustuv.com/). I obtained my B.S. (Honors) degree in Computer Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/). My research interests lie in **LLM Reasoning & Agents Harness**, and **Human–AI Interaction**. My work spans **LLM-based agents, H-AI tacit understanding/cooridination, and behavioral signal analysis**, and I build full-stack systems to study how humans and AI collaborate in structured/unstructured settings. My most recent focus is on **self-evolving** agents for industrial-intent.

Before pivoting into AI/HAI, I worked in security and networked systems, including leading **CAPSID**, a privacy-preserving UAV identification protocol published at **ACM CCS 2024**, and collaborating over **Automatic FarmNetes**, which led to a publication at **ACM SEC 2025**.
