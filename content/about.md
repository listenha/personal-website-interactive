Hi! I'm Yueshen Li, a MSCS student at the University of Illinois Urbana–Champaign advised by Prof. [Koustuv Saha](https://koustuv.com/). I'm broadly interested in **Human–AI Interaction**, **LLM Reasoning & Interpretation**, and **AI systems that understand people**. My work spans **tacit understanding, compatibility modeling, behavioral signal analysis, and LLM-based agents**, and I build full-stack systems to study how humans and AI collaborate in natural, unstructured settings.

Before pivoting into HCI/HAI, I worked in security and networked systems, including leading **CAPSID**, a privacy-preserving UAV identification protocol published at **ACM CCS 2024**, and collaborating over **Automatic FarmNetes**, which led to a publication at **ACM SEC 2025**.
